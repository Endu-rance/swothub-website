Core Operations

What We Do

SWOTHub LTD operates through four specialized business divisions:

  • Real Estate / Construction Services: Roads, sub-base, towers, schools, housing & govt. builds.

  • ICT / Enterprise Solutions: Cloud, cybersecurity, big data, compliance, ERP, digital transformation & workflow automation.

  • EdTech Solutions: School management systems, e-learning, curriculum tools.

  • Government & Public Sector Solutions: E-governance systems, citizen engagement tools, and digital reporting platforms.

Together, these divisions form a comprehensive ecosystem that enables schools, enterprises, and governments to work smarter and grow sustainably.
